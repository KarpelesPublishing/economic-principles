# Economic Principles guide

This skill is the front door to 99 chapter skills, one per chapter of *The Encyclopedia of Economic Principals* by Jason Karpeles. Each chapter skill applies the chapter's worked examples to the user's own numbers and points to a Jupyter notebook that reproduces the book's figures.

## How to route a question

1. Restate the question as a decision or a mechanism: who chooses, what they trade off, and what number the user wants (a price, a quantity, a threshold, a probability, a present value).
2. Find the part below whose theme fits, then the chapter whose principles name the mechanism. Search the principle lists for the user's own words (for example "adverse selection", "Taylor rule", "tax incidence").
3. Open that chapter's skill (path in the table) and follow its steps. Ask for the inputs it lists; where the user has none, use the book's example values and say so.
4. If two chapters fit, prefer the one whose principle matches the question most directly, and mention the other in one line. A question that spans parts (for example a tax on a monopolist) can use one skill per step.
5. Say which numbers are the user's, which are assumptions, and which come from the book's constructed teaching examples. The examples are hypothetical; they do not measure any real market.

Each chapter skill lives at `../ep-chNNN-<slug>/SKILL.md`, and its notebook at `../../notebooks/chNNN-<slug>.ipynb` (relative to the skill's folder).
